# Competitors addendum: small-business and freelancer contract review

Date: 2026-11-13. Added during the brief session after the question "is there a tool for small
business owners that evaluates supplier and vendor contracts?" Answer: yes, several, two of them
free from household brands. This fills the gap `existing-tools.md` flagged under "Free or
consumer/freelancer-focused contract review tools."

Method: four web searches plus a direct read of two Rocket Lawyer pages. Everything else is from
search summaries and vendor pages. Prices are vendor-reported unless noted.

## Direct reads

### Rocket Lawyer, Rocket Copilot Contract Review
- Launched 12 Aug 2025. Headline: "Empowering Small Business Owners to Negotiate, Sign and
  Enforce Contracts." Described as "a new, free, AI-powered legal experience that helps small
  business owners confidently evaluate and sign contracts."
- What it does: "Upload any contract to see if there are any red flags or missing information."
  It will "highlight key terms, flag potential risks, and share what might need a second look
  before you sign."
- Price: free with email registration. Attorney follow-up needs a Rocket Legal+ membership
  (7-day trial). Legal Advice Session "starts at just $149."
- Not mentioned on either page: quoting the uploaded text, tying a flag to a sentence or
  location, suggested edits or redlines, a risk score, AI answering follow-up questions (that
  was promised for "later this year" in the Aug 2025 release). A May 2026 release describes an
  enhanced beta pairing AI with human legal professionals.
- Contract types: none named. "Upload any contract." Vendor and supplier agreements are not
  mentioned.
- Disclaimer: "Rocket Lawyer Incorporated isn't a law firm or a substitute for one."
- Accuracy claim: AI "trained on 16 years of Rocket Lawyer's proprietary legal analysis data."
  The landing page says "over 15 years." The figures differ.
- Data: documents "are encrypted, processed in a secure environment, and never used to train
  AI models."
- URLs: https://rocketlawyer.com/contract-review ;
  https://www.rocketlawyer.com/newsroom/rocket-lawyer-launches-rocket-copilot-contract-review

## What this means for Redline

- The small-business owner on the paying side (vendor and supplier contracts) is the most
  crowded corner: two free tools from brands they already know, and no pain evidence in our
  research for that slice. Redline should not aim there. D1 in `CONTEXT.md` was tightened to the
  occasional freelancer as a result.
- "Upload any contract" covers freelancers too, so the audience is not the differentiator. The
  trust mechanics are: exact-sentence citation, red lines as the whole rulebook, no false alarms,
  a receipt instead of reassurance. No competitor page read here advertises any of them.
- The brief should name Rocket Copilot Contract Review and the general chatbot as the free
  incumbents. The claim to beat is "flag potential risks" with no visible source.
